Google Ads volumes are estimates for web search, not Chrome Web Store searches. Close variants can share volumes; do not sum them. Ads competition is not organic ranking difficulty.

Title: **Short Copy - URL Shortener & Copy Link**. Keep the brand first, then the category and action. Lead the summary with shortening and copying. Explain the shortcut and no-account workflow in the description. Avoid promises of rankings and features the extension does not have.

Live search results showed category-led titles and shortcut/copy workflows in useClick, Copy URL, Alternative Copy and Short Link. This supports clear category language, not keyword repetition.
